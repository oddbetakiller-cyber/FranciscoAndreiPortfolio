## 📸 Where to Put Your Profile Picture

Put your picture in the **`assets`** folder:

1. Copy your picture and paste it inside:  
   👉 `adet studeprofile/assets/`
2. Rename your picture to:  
   👉 **`profile.jpg`**
3. Refresh `index.html` in your browser!

> 💡 **Tip:** A square photo (e.g. 500x500 px or 800x800 px) or centered portrait photo looks best inside the circular glowing frame. You can also click the **"Preview Photo Now"** button directly on the page to preview any photo instantly before copying it!
